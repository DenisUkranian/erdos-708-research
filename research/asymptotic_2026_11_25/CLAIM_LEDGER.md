# Chronological claim ledger — research after the 24 November archive

**Policy.** The statements below are a research log, not a theorem list. Some conversational arguments were incomplete or used estimates outside their proved scope. The labels "component", "conditional", "candidate" and "gap" are intentional. This ledger supersedes any interpretation of those conversations as an unconditional proof of the general asymptotic. Reported numerical test counts without preserved scripts/logs are not used as evidence.

| Route / claim in the discussion | Present audit |
|---|---|
| Adaptive demand core \(E_*=\{p\le\sqrt M:D_p>n\log M/(\sqrt M\log\log M)\}\) | Small-demand primes outside it have aggregate demand \(O(n/\log\log M)\) by Chebyshev; one factor \(>\sqrt M\) per source. An arbitrary exact-profile core cannot always be covered in \(n+o(n)\) positions. |
| Pair-product counterexample to independent core budget | Valid construction: take all \(p_i p_j\) for \(i\le j\), primes in \([x,1.2x]\); \(n=k(k+1)/2\), \(M=\max p_i^2\). Choose interval centered at a CRT integer with exactly two units of each \(p_i\); off-center positions carry at most one active unit, since radius \(<x^2\). Exact minimum \(1+2n-2k\). This refutes an independent \(n+o(n)\) core cover, not coefficient two. |
| Fixed \(\alpha\), separated support with growing exact core \(k\) | Candidate extension of the previous discounted certificate: log(k+2)=o(log M/(loglog M)^{t/(t+1)}), \(t=\lfloor1/\alpha\rfloor\). Requires precise sieve comparison and treatment of pure core. Do not infer arbitrary interleaved support. |
| Sparse hot set: \(Q=\{p\le M^{1/(k+1)}:w_p>1/k\}\), \(\lambda_Q=o(1)\), \(M\lambda_Q^k=O_k(n)\) | Component: two-term divisor Bonferroni, \(k\) fixed, \(|H|=O(n)=o(M)\); \(k\)-fold products below \(M^{k/(k+1)}\). For the small-mass extraction step, each significant prime tends to infinity by the \(p^k\) bank. |
| Capped kernel \(S=\sum_{p\in Q}\min(w_p v_p,1)\) and truncated Möbius transfer of \((S-1)_+\) | Candidate; verify degree-\(k\) remainder bound and uniform constants before use. |
| Arbitrary larger weights plus \(w_p>1/3\) for active \(p\le M^{1/4}\) | Candidate. A pair maximum inequality \(\sum_i \max_{j\ne i}(u_i+u_j-s)_+\le2(\sum_i u_i-s)_+\) for \(0\le u_i\le s\) is a valid local ingredient, but repeated-prime rows and joint payments require explicit proof. The conversational 28,728/1,281 checks have no preserved executable results here. |
| Downward-closed smooth-profile relative transfer | Conditional input: geometric measure, Rankin tail, and fundamental/upper-bound sieve. Need real-length intervals, uniformity in the excluded families, and quantitative error. It is not a general upward-set transfer. |
| Near-quadratic \(M\ge n^{2-\eta_\delta}\) and shrinking-log exponent extension | Candidate. Earlier archived near-quadratic result is a separate input. The new proof used two classes of carriers; the complement-cold condition and number of discarded two-hot-factor sources must be retained. Any restriction to a global optimizer must not be silently upgraded to all sets. |
| Weighted far-intersection and clipping bounds | Audited component lemmas, in COMPONENT_LEMMAS.md. They remove the square of the carrier count, but the normalized total density \(W\) remains. |
| Binary-weight truncated sign tree | Candidate / historical. The simple stopping-rule description is not a complete derivation of a Rosser-type minorant; negative branch handling and all active target prime powers must be checked. The older two_bin_certificate package contains explicit obstacles to integer-weight Rosser extensions. |
| Sparse optimum in one fixed weight interval \(1/k<w_p\le1/(k-1)\) | Candidate. The proposed largest-active-prime balance argument requires strict target surplus in at least one nonempty level and a justified bound for overlapping witness multisets. The one-band proof cannot be invoked after price rounding. |
| Sparse mixture of weights bounded below by fixed \(\eta\) | Candidate. Partition into regular/rare primes and two kinds of crossing banks is a promising route. It requires a uniform construction of the regular part, disjoint first-crossing classes, rare continuation transfer and a strict row contradiction. Removing small prices changes optimality and cannot preserve tight-row equalities by assumption. |
| Count of 600 weight models / 720 level models | Exploratory conversational claim only; no reproducible artifacts identified. Not an analytic proof. |
| Small-price removal / finite alphabet | Component subject to the stated uniform moment inequalities: exact active row balances yield \(\sum_{p:w_p>0}D_p=O(n(1+\log(M/n)))\) for \(F\ge2n\). Thus removal or downward quantization by \(o(1/(1+\log(M/n)))\) costs \(o(n)\) in source load. Rounding need not preserve optimality. |
| Arbitrarily small-gap diffuse level transfer | Candidate proof uses conditional means \(V(d)+\mu_{[p,T/d]}\), first crossing, exact lower-prime profile, and quotient sieve. The comparison must be relative to the source tail, not just absolute \(o(M)\). |
| Rare-divisor averaging | Conditional consequence if the preceding level comparison is established uniformly for real interval lengths: partition a length-M interval into \(c\) real intervals of length \(M/c\); source counts for \(V(a/c)\) are at most \(1/c\) of the full target count times the comparison factor. Sum over \(c\le M\). This does not pay a deleted factor's own valuation demand. |
| Order by log(p)/w_p | Component: for first carrier \(d\) crossing \(t\), \(X(d)\le t+1\), and the weighted-average order gives \(\log d/\log a\le X(d)/X(a)\) after ignoring zero-price factors. If \(X(a)>t+1+\delta\), then \(M/d\ge M^{\delta/(t+1+\delta)}\). A preceding prime \(q\) of weight \(\le\eta\) obeys \(q\le(M/d)^{\eta/\delta}\). Exact classes are defined by full earlier-prime occurrences, including equality of order ties. |
| Excluding a polynomial number of substantial weights and extension to demand core \(E=\{p:D_p>p\}\) | Candidate. Requires simultaneous nonlinear comparison with the outside-prime terms at shorter lengths \(M/r\), uniform level integration and correct handling of sources with low/zero prices. The external core cardinality restriction is not automatic. |
| Two residue classes from user's research update | Preserved as a reported result: short-bank and ordered-prime-bank constructions were combined without repeated positions under their own hypotheses, using weighted intersections. Exact hypotheses/proof not accessible in the conversation, so no theorem beyond that scope is recorded. |
| Long-bank union bound without quadratic number of carriers | Audited conditional deduction in COMPONENT_LEMMAS.md. |
| redlydi comparison and local LCM-loss budget | Audited component in COMPONENT_LEMMAS.md and provenance in REDLYDI_COMPARISON.md. |
| Random truncated prime profiles | Pointwise exclusivity proved; exact averaged lower bound still needs proof. The original claim for all \(\omega(a)\le(1-\varepsilon)\log\log M\), extension after arbitrary reserved cover, and polynomial smooth cutoff are NOT promoted to theorems. For reserve repairs use a joint construction, not independent overlapping products. |
| Capped large-prime valuation certificate | Audited separated-support class in COMPONENT_LEMMAS.md, with fixed reciprocal bound, long banks and \(O(\log\log\log M)\) distinct larger primes. Its growing Hall reserve is a valid component. |
| Rare continuations \(a=hqu\) | Conditional matching/counting result; residual demand of \(qu\) is not generally one prime unit. |
| Linear fraction of all extremal sources must have several large significant factors; fixed gap in their log scales | Candidate, not established by the separated-support theorem. A prime-band cleanup may discard weighted demand, but must account for the common budget at the remaining levels and for diffuse weights. |
