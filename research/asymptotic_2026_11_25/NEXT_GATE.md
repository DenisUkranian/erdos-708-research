# Remaining gate and research priorities

For \(X_w(t)=\sum_p w_pv_p(t)\), \(0\le w_p\le1\), the full-tail surplus is
\[
 J_{2+\delta}(w;M,I)=\sum_{a\le M}(X_w(a)-2-\delta)_+
 -\sum_{b\in I}(X_w(b)-1)_+ .
\]
At a positive global maximum the full strict tail \(A=\{a:X_w(a)>2+\delta\}\) and its paired boxed LP optimum share tight active rows. Previously archived uniform tail bounds give weighted demand \(O_\delta(n)\), and uniform integrability of the source loads. The known Wang raw-to-integer transfer and minimal obstruction reduction remain the route from an unrestricted raw bound to \(g(n)\le(2+o(1))n\).

**Sufficient next theorem:** for each fixed \(\delta>0\), a uniform bound \(J_{2+\delta}\le C_\delta\) over \(M,I,w\), or exclusion of all positive global maximizers with unbounded full-tail size. A proof restricted to a fixed density or separated support does not suffice.

The constructive alternative is to choose representations \(a=d_ap_ar_a\) (or another carrier with a fully budgeted remaining requirement), build locally injective matches, and prove an aggregate prime-unit LCM loss \(o(n)\) for all unhandled sources. The reserve-Hall and clipping estimates permit joint accounting across many groups. The difficult case still includes interleaved prime scales, unbounded support, and diffuse-plus-substantial mixtures. The remaining demand must be paid once, not independently allocated to multiple target budgets.

A proof must (i) specify the actual source set or full tail, (ii) preserve the same paired optimum whenever exact row balance is used, (iii) establish a global target payment before summation, (iv) control all uncovered prime units after matching, and (v) justify the uniform error relative to n. The recent research is useful but there is no justified estimate of proximity in time or probability to a full solution.
