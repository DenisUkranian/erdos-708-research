# Uniform comparison of valuation profiles on a subpower number of primes

Let S={p_1<...<p_s} be any nonempty finite prime set. Let q_i be the i-th prime, y=q_s, and u=log M/log y. For n>0 encode its S-profile by K(n)=∏q_i^{v_{p_i}(n)}. Independently sample nonnegative G_i with P(G_i≥h)=p_i^{-h}, and put Z=∏q_i^{G_i}. For an arbitrary interval I of M consecutive positive integers take b uniform on I. There are absolute c,C>0 such that, whenever u is sufficiently large,

    d_TV(K(b),Z) ≤ C exp(−c min(log M,u log(2u))).

In particular the bound tends to zero for any s=M^{o(1)}, without any restriction on the largest real prime p_s, the shift, or the event being tested. For comparison with the prefix [1,M], multiply the bound by two. The empty prime support gives identically zero discrepancy.

## Banded inclusion-exclusion proof

Group the compressed primes into bands y^{2^{−j−1}}<q_i≤y^{2^{−j}}, starting with j=0. There are O(1+log log(3y)) nonempty bands. The elementary Mertens estimate gives a uniform upper bound C_0 on the sum of 1/p_i in each band, since p_i≥q_i. The band product V_j=∏(1−1/p_i) has an absolute positive lower bound.

Take r_j to be the least even integer at least u/64+j. For an integer z, let U_j and L_j be the inclusion-exclusion polynomials for no p_i in that band dividing z through degrees r_j and r_j+1, respectively. Thus U_j≥1_{no hit}≥L_j, U_j≥0, and U_j−L_j is the degree r_j+1 elementary symmetric sum of the divisibility indicators. Telescoping products of upper bounds and actual indicators gives the simultaneous minorant

    P = ∏ U_j − ∑_j (U_j−L_j) ∏_{k≠j} U_k.

In the independent divisibility model its mean is at least V(1−O(exp(−c u log(2u)))), where V=∏V_j. Indeed the band gap has mean at most C_0^{r_j+1}/(r_j+1)!. Dividing by the lower bound on V_j and summing over j gives O(exp(−c u log(2u))). The mean of ∏U_j is at least V and at most V times the product of one plus the relative band errors; the latter product is 1+O(exp(−c u log(2u))). Independence then gives the stated minorant estimate.

Write D(d)=∏p_i^{v_{q_i}(d)} for d supported on the compressed primes. For exact K(b)=d require D(d)|b and no p_i dividing b/D(d). Expand the minorant at the quotient. Its monomials correspond to squarefree compressed integers e with

    e ≤ y^{∑ 2^{−j}(r_j+1)} ≤ M^{1/8}

for all sufficiently large u (the exponent sum is at most u/32+8). A monomial's coefficient has magnitude at most one plus the number of bands by unique factorization. The associated actual modulus D(d)D(e) has a count in I differing from M/(D(d)D(e)) by at most one, even when the modulus exceeds M. For every compressed d≤M^{1/4} this proves

    P(K(b)=d) ≥ [V/D(d)](1−O(exp(−c u log(2u))))
                 − O(M^{−7/8}(1+log log(3y))).

Here V/D(d) is precisely P(Z=d).

To bound the omitted model mass, set t=min(1/2,log(2u)/(2 log y)). By p_i≥q_i and t≤1/2,

    log E Z^t ≤ C ∑_{q≤y prime}(q^t−1)/q ≤ C' y^t.

For the last bound write q^t−1=∫_0^t (log q)q^v dv. Chebyshev's θ(X)≪X and partial summation give ∑_{q≤y} (log q) q^{v−1}≪ y^v+(y^v−1)/v. Integration costs O(y^t), since (e^h−1)/h≤e^h for h≥0. Thus P(Z>M^{1/4})≤exp(−(t/4)log M+C'√(2u)). Since u≤log M/log 2, this is O(exp(−c min(log M,u log(2u)))) for large u. Sum the positive deficits P(Z=d)−P(K(b)=d) over d≤M^{1/4}, and bound the rest by the model tail. This yields the claim, as the aggregate counting error is O(M^{−5/8}(1+log log(3y))).

## Consequences for Erdős 708

For a cut T⊂I define its trapped divisors to be those d≤M all of whose multiples in I lie in T. Suppose the minimal generators of this divisibility ideal involve altogether s=M^{o(1)} distinct primes, chosen even after seeing I and T. Extend the ideal to the periodic set U generated by these same divisors, with U∩I⊆T. Its independent-model probability is its periodic density. Conditioning an independent geometric model for *all* primes up to M on its product being ≤M makes its law proportional to 1/d for d≤M. Harris's inequality for the increasing event of lying in U and the decreasing conditioning event bounds the harmonic probability of U by its independent-model probability. The profile theorem compares that probability to the actual interval density with error at most the displayed TV bound. Thus the trapped-divisor harmonic mass divided by H_M is at most |T|/M plus that error. This is uniform over such adaptive cuts, regardless of the size of the joint period or the number of generators. Equivalently, a sequence of cuts with a positive harmonic excess bounded away from zero must use at least M^δ distinct prime bases for some δ>0 eventually (with δ depending on the excess).

For a source set A⊂[2,M] of size n, one may also match exact S-profiles between rows and targets, except for m≤2CM exp(−c min(log M,u log(2u))) rows. The distinct representatives pay the entire S-part of their matched rows. For each prime p, repair still-unpaid rows containing p using at most their count in fresh positions: the level sets p^j|b are nested and the full interval has enough multiples at every level. This gives

    H(A,I) ≤ n + ∑_{a∈A} ω_{p∉S}(a)
                 + [m/log 2] [log(M/m)+log(1+log M)],

with zero extra cost for m=0. Indeed ∑_{a≤M}2^{ω(a)} = ∑_{d≤M} μ²(d) floor(M/d) ≤ M(1+log M); Jensen bounds the sum of ω on the m exceptional rows. This allows a subpower prime support whenever the displayed repair term is o(n) and the outside-atom sum is ≤n+o(n). It does not prove that arbitrary source families or cuts have this structure.

The coordinate compression and source-profile matching construction already appear in [redlydi, interval transport, Theorems 5–6](https://github.com/redlydi/Erdos708-Research/blob/32f397f8fdc00d0e9c73242d8510c541267a30db/reports/erdos708-interval-transport%20%281%29.md). The [polylogarithmic smooth-profile theorem](../cofactor_flow_2026_11_26/SMOOTH_PROFILES.md) gives the sharp error exponent for the initial prime segment in that regime. The banded sieve here permits the entire range s=M^{o(1)} against the independent valuation model. The proof is a version of the fundamental-lemma sieve underlying the classical Kubilius model. No priority claim is made.
