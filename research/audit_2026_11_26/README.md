## Project materials

[Density proof and exact replay outputs](../density_2026_11_26/README.md) · [Historical finite files and outstanding manifest entries](FINITE_INVENTORY.md) · [Constructive matching bound and open target](MATCHING.md). The eight Node programs in the density directory were rerun, including 12,443,623 + 470,027 boxes at the final threshold. This verifies the arithmetic in the implementation. The analytic reduction and its uniform error remain written arguments. The older density ZIPs preserve the two intermediate versions. The every-density lower bound concerns a strict linear excess above n, not above 2n.

Recovered g(9) replay: see g9-replay.txt. The separate upper-12 program remains partial. The previously discussed near-maximal reciprocal-mass criterion lacks a recovered standalone proof with all parameters defined; do not cite it as an unconditional result. Earlier claims and failed transfers are recorded in the [claim ledger](../asymptotic_2026_11_25/CLAIM_LEDGER.md).

Additional references: [Formal Conjectures statement PR 4388](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/pull/4388) and [independent Chen build, corrected hinge obstruction and all-size lower construction](https://www.erdosproblemaday.com/day/708-chen-12n-refereed-and-a-k2t-window-at-every-size). The latter reports a lower bound floor(3n/2)−1 for n ≥ 4. A statement formalization and an endpoint proof have different scopes.
