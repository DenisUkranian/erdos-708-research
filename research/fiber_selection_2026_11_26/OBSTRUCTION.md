# Exact obstruction to full unions followed by singleton repairs

Write J=[1,M], I=(x,x+M] in positive integers, n=|A| and M=max A. A set V⊆J is upward closed if a∈V and a|b≤M imply b∈V. These are exactly the unions of progressions d|u, with no bound on the number of generators. The sufficient budget from the [previous round](../twelve_routes_2026_11_26/MATCHING_AND_CUTS.md) contains the term

    C(V)=|V|+Σ_{a∈A outside V} ω(a).

We disprove a uniform 2n+o(n) bound for the minimum of this term. This does not disprove the conditional implication for a hypothetical hard instance, or any bound on the original product optimum.

## A lower bound applying to every union

Take a prime q and any A⊆{a∈J:a≡1 mod q}. For every V as above,

    C(V) ≥ Σ_{a∈A} min(floor(M/a),q,ω(a)).                          (1)

Every a∈A∩V forces ka∈V for 1≤k≤min(q,floor(M/a)). For fixed k the forced points are distinct, and different k are distinguished by their residues modulo q, including k=q. For a outside V use the singleton charge ω(a). This proves (1) simultaneously for every V.

For A={1+qj:1≤j≤n}, at every fixed q the liminf of min C(V)/n is at least H_q=Σ_{k≤q}1/k. For primes q→∞ with q asymptotic to log n, min C(V)/n tends to infinity. To prove both statements, fix K≤q. For each 1≤k≤K the number of a≤M/k with ω(a)≥k is (1/k−o(1))n. Indeed j ranges through n/k+O(1) consecutive integers. On any finite list of primes different from q, the divisibility indicators converge jointly to independent Bernoulli variables of means 1/p, by CRT and complete-period counting. In the growing-q case the list is fixed first and eventually omits q automatically; the residue of q may vary, with the same uniform period error. By divergence of Σ1/p and variance at most the mean, the chance of fewer than k prime divisors from the list can be made arbitrarily small. Sum the first K layers of (1), then in the growing case allow K to increase. Such sequences exist, for example choose n=floor(exp(q)). At q=5 this gives H_5=137/60>2 already at fixed density 1/5. These sources have gcd one.

## Covering the same family

Select in I the points congruent to 1 modulo q. Their number is at most n+1. For every power p^j the extra congruence is either inconsistent or a single residue class modulo its lcm with q. The two equal-length intervals differ by at most one candidate at that level. For p≤n add at most one further position per prime, using the nesting of its level sets; when a level is deficient the entire interval contains enough multiples to repair. When M<n², each row has at most one prime above n, to exponent one. Pay at most B further points, where B counts such rows. Thus H(A,I)≤n+1+π(n)+B for every shift; H denotes the level-cover optimum and dominates ordinary product cost τ.

In fact B≪n log(2q)/log(n/q) for q fixed or q asymptotic to log n. Write a=ph with p>n. Then h≤q, q∤h, and p≡h^(−1) mod q. The Brun–Titchmarsh inequality at X=M/h bounds the prime count by 2X/[(q−1)log(X/q)]≪ n/[h log(n/q)]. Sum over h≤q. In particular **H≤n+O(n log log n/log n)** for q asymptotic to log n, even though min C(V)/n→∞. For fixed q the one-level discrepancy alone gives H≤n+1+π(M)=n+O_q(n/log n). These statements are upper bounds, with no claim about exact worst-case minima for this source.

The Brun–Titchmarsh input appears in [Lenstra and Pomerance, Lemma 11.1](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/aks240817.pdf).

## Exact finite minimization

Use a bipartite network with source capacities ω(a) at row vertices, a large-capacity edge from each row to each of its multiples in J, and a unit edge from each target to the sink. Take the large capacity greater than Σ_A ω(a)+M. By an integral minimum cut the flow value is exactly min C(V). Indeed a chosen set of rows forces its union of multiples. If this union also contains an unchosen source, adding that source to the chosen rows has no new target cost. Conversely an optimal upward V can be replaced by the union generated by A∩V. This test optimizes even over generator lists of unrestricted length.

The finite test also includes issues of scale. Take all a≡1 mod 5 of rank at least three up to floor(3001/3), and add 3001. Each small a gives at least three in (1). The available union budgets and the obstruction are checked with exact integer flow. For a product upper witness the script transports the corresponding two residue-class pieces in the target and completes any level deficits. The asymptotic statements above use their own proofs.
