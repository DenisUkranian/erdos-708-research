DIRECTED GLOBAL BANK-UNION AUDIT

Purpose
-------
Test an intermediate global lemma, not the original covering minimum g(n):
for clipped rational weights with X(a)>2, must the total weighted valuation
on the FULL prime-deletion bank union exceed the source weighted valuation?

Method
------
For a fixed actual interval represented by compatible prime-power CRT
phases, one integral maximum-closure computation optimizes over every
subset of the strictly-hot source candidates. It is not a sample of source
sets. Source -> cofactor -> target arcs enforce the actual union of all
a/q-multiples for every distinct q dividing every selected source.

The final implementation takes the maximal source side of an optimal cut
(the complement of vertices reaching the sink in the residual graph).
This also detects nonempty equality witnesses. Returning only the minimal
source side could conceal a nonempty zero-profit optimum.

All target valuations are exact, not truncated. For each source prime the
specified CRT modulus is a sufficiently large power that no target offset
is zero modulo that power. The independent witness checker uses Python's
standard library only. Search requires NumPy and SciPy. The script checks
the signed-32-bit capacity limits of SciPy's maximum-flow implementation.

Final directed cases
--------------------
All have interval length M=20000. Small prime residues are aligned at a
center. In "spread" mode large-prime residue classes favor newly uncovered
positions. In "park" mode they favor positions free of the small primes
and may reuse those positions across different large primes.

1. Weights 1/4 on 2 and 3, weight 1 on other active source primes;
   spread phases; 9650 strictly-hot source candidates.
2. Weights 51/100 on primes <=sqrt(M), weight 1 above sqrt(M);
   park phases; 10895 strictly-hot source candidates.
3. Weight 1 on active source primes >=5, zero on 2 and 3;
   park phases; 3244 strictly-hot source candidates.

In all three final computations, the maximal optimal source selection was
empty and its profit was zero. The earlier *_maximal.json suffix identifies
these final records. No counterexample to the weighted bank-union claim was
found. These computations do NOT establish the universal intermediate
lemma, the asymptotic conjecture, or the existence of a minimal obstruction.
The preceding developmental probes are retained as intermediate output and
are not presented as independent mathematical results.

Reproduction (from this directory)
----------------------------------
python joint_bank_closure.py --M 20000 --profile light23 --mode spread
python joint_bank_closure.py --M 20000 --profile large --mode park
python joint_bank_closure.py --M 20000 --profile rough --mode park

Standard-library-only validation of a saved witness description:
python -I joint_bank_closure.py --verify probe_M20000_large_park_maximal.json

The verifier checks the arithmetic witness and actual CRT interval. An
empty witness is merely consistent data; its validation alone is not a
standalone certificate of maximum-flow optimality. The negative search
result is reported as a computation, not as a formally checked theorem.

The coordinatewise bank-product statement is separately disproved by the
exact lifted Hall certificate in ../combinatorial. That counterexample
does not violate the high-source-load weighted statement tested here.
