1. THE ORIGINAL PROBLEM AND THE COMMON FRACTIONAL ENDPOINT
For n distinct integers A, M=max A, every interval I of M consecutive
positive integers should contain a small set whose product is divisible
by the product of A. The work also uses a fixed ambient M >= max A during
source deletions, without shortening the target interval.

For clipped prime weights 0 <= w_p <= 1, write
  X(t) = sum_p w_p v_p(t),       H_X(b) = (X(b)-1)_+,
  J_c(w) = sum_{a<=M}(X(a)-c)_+ - sum_{b in I} H_X(b).
A uniform J_(2+delta)(w) <= C_delta, for every fixed delta>0, is a
sufficient endpoint. Wang's cited raw fractional-to-integer transfer
already supplies the passage to the desired integer asymptotic; that
transfer is not the missing step of the latest programme.

The older exact fixed-n objective keeps M as a required source:
  X(M) + Top_(n-1){X(a):2<=a<M} - sum_I H_X.
This is an exact maximization over source sets with max A=M, but choosing
a top set at arbitrary weights does not itself imply paired optimality.

3. GENERAL REDUCTIONS NOW RECORDED
At a genuine global maximum of J_c, c=2+delta, choose the entire strict
tail A={a<=M:X(a)>c}, n=|A|. Then these are the SAME A,w and a paired
primal optimum z, with F=L_raw(A,I):
  max_w J_c = max_S (L_raw(S,I)-c|S|),
  F=cn+J_c,   z_b=1 when X(b)>1,
  sum_I z_b v_p(b)=sum_A v_p(a) for every active prime p.
These balances concern whole raw prime rows, including every power; they
are not separate prime-power-level equalities. They must not be moved to
an unrelated source set, modified weight profile, or nonadditive model.

The global-surplus and dot reports record bounded reciprocal mean,
bounded total source load O_delta(n), and exponential integrability
relative to the full tail's own cardinality. In particular, for a subset
of t sources, its excess weighted demand is at most
  2 B_delta t log(2n/t).
Thus o(n) exceptional sources have o(n) weighted demand on this class.
All source prime factors, even zero-price factors, are O_delta(n), and
there are O_delta(n/log n) active prime rows. The relevant positive-
extremum range is cn < M < 16 n^2.

The dot audit records a general near-quadratic exclusion: for each fixed
delta,kappa>0, there is no sequence of positive global maxima with
n->infinity and M >= kappa n^2. The remaining unbounded branch is
M=o(n^2). This statement is about true full-tail global maxima, with no
assumed small support or prescribed weight profile.

4. THE LATEST PROOF LADDERS — ESTABLISHED STEPS
The newest report is ep708_ladder_20261123/erdos708_proof_ladders.tex.
It spends part of the asymptotic slack on complements of load at most
  tau=1+delta/2,  gamma=delta/2.
A source a may use target b if X(a/gcd(a,b)) <= tau. If a single target
budget serves source fractions theta_a and the total unserved fraction
is t=sum(1-theta_a), with extra accounting error E, then
  J_c <= 2 B_delta t log(2n/t) - gamma(n-t) + E.
A sufficiently small FIXED fraction depending on delta suffices for a
contradiction; every rung need not demand an o(n) exceptional set.

Adaptive-Hall reductions: a maximum-deficit source set is an upset under
divisibility. Its complementary cut obeys an exact arithmetic closure
condition. A hot common divisor can pay its entire cluster with an extra
unit per source. Every source has either a pure substantial-price carrier
or a numerically small hot carrier, with a controlled residual light-prime
cutoff. High-light source fibres have a small normalized demand density,
including all repeated and inactive cofactors. These facts do not yet
control overlap among different banks.

Diffuse-transfer reductions: for a raw load V with individual prices at
most eta and bounded reciprocal mean, a one-sided concentration bound
holds uniformly in EVERY shifted quotient interval of length N:
  sum_J (mu_N-V)_+ <= psi(eta) N,  psi(eta)->0.
There is also a prefix L1 bound. Exact original row balances turn these
into a general vanishing small-price-demand estimate on each positive-
density branch n/M >= rho_0>0. Conditional exact-profile cells retain the
correct sieve density when their genuine length gap is available.

Most importantly for sparse tails, demand-normalized common clipping
transfers one target budget with error O_delta(Lambda psi(eta) n), rather
than O(M). A stronger cluster normalization retains the unit reserve of
one-bank credit. These estimates apply without a prescribed prime count
or weight profile. They do NOT show that the model budget captures enough
source demand.

5. PRECISE CURRENT OPEN OBLIGATIONS
The preferred cluster formulation sets
  S_2=sum_A(X(a)-2),
chooses first-crossing roots g, and normalizes each cluster by
  alpha_g = [sum_{a in A_g}(X(a)-2)] / [sum_{b in I:g|b}H_X(b)].
The proved identity and norm bound use
  R(b)=sum_{g|b}alpha_g,
  sum_I H_X(b)R(b)=S_2,
  sum_g alpha_g M/g=O_delta(n).
A diffuse model H_model and a common clipped capture functional Q_Lambda
then satisfy
  J_c <= S_2-delta n-Q_Lambda(H_model)
         +O_delta(Lambda psi(eta)n).
The still UNPROVED arithmetic target is
  Q_(Lambda_delta)(H_model) >= S_2-delta n/2,
uniformly on the genuine positive-global-optimum class. Lambda_delta must
be fixed before eta is made small, or a quantitative dependence must prove
Lambda(eta) psi(eta)->0. An uncontrolled parameter choice is circular.

An alternative sufficient target is a small-deficit relaxed Hall bound
on the exact arithmetic cuts, retaining the true original row balances.
A joint truncated lcm-energy estimate or an all-occurrence stopping
certificate might supply it. No such terminal inequality is proved in
the archive. Pairwise cold gcds alone do not control all multiway charges.

6. RESTRICTED RESULTS AND NEGATIVE EVIDENCE
The earlier reports contain valid, explicitly restricted exact or
asymptotic classes: rough/cofactor-support conditions, fixed or controlled
cores, and separated prime bands. The three/four-external-occurrence
certificate uses an actual small periodic core and external primes above
M^(1/5); later repairs retain repeated primes and relative row errors.
It is not a normalization theorem for arbitrary optimal weights.

The exact numerical certificates and CRT constructions refute specified
stronger shortcuts, including coordinatewise bank domination,
same-threshold transfer, independent root budgets, and unamplified
uniform routing. They do not refute the original conjecture or the
unproved lemma restricted to true positive global extrema. Finite solver
searches and matching probes are diagnostic evidence only; the latest
work computes no new g(n).
