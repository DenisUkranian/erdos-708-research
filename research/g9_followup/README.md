## Important correction

Earlier smooth-source computations pruned with budget-13 factor thresholds (e.g. 12 and 13) and were not complete proofs for budget 12. The later corrected smooth package explicitly regenerates the appropriate domain. Do not cite the superseded 44,193-vector domain as proof of the upper-12 smooth branch. The upper-13 proof is a separate, valid finite-case route with its own domain. The two-prime reduction does not imply the global 12-bound.

## Additional prior descent archive

The library also contains erdos708_descent_attempt.zip, SHA-256 586297ef4c10a6949ff990eca811944a7f729eda2c50e7bee2b6407956e4cc44. It documents an arithmetic cold-quotient obstruction, a hot-root union discrepancy above square-root scale, a nonarithmetic uniquely optimized model obstructing localization from row balances alone, and the direction of a phase-averaging inequality. These are obstructions to intermediate claims, not to the original problem. Some overlapping content already appears in research/two_bin_certificate; preserve the original package separately and avoid claiming the descent theorem is proved.

The full source ZIPs (including binaries, data, and original logs) are distinct from this index. The archive hashes let the original recovered packages be identified and compared without falsely asserting that every large binary has been transferred to GitHub.
